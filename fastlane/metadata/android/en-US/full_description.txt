SecureBlue Chat is a two-phone messenger that runs entirely over Bluetooth. There is no internet permission, no account and no server: the phones talk to each other directly.

ENCRYPTED BY DEFAULT
Every message is sealed with AES-256-GCM. The key comes from a PIN that both people type, stretched with PBKDF2-HMAC-SHA256 over a random per-session salt. If the PINs do not match, the handshake fails and no message is ever shown.

HOW A SESSION WORKS
* One phone taps Wait, the other taps Scan and picks it from the list.
* Both enter the same 4-8 digit PIN.
* An encrypted challenge-response proves the keys match.
* The chat opens with a solid lock icon in the toolbar.

WHILE CHATTING
* Timestamps on every message.
* A live typing indicator, also encrypted.
* Long-press a bubble to copy or delete it.
* Clear the whole conversation behind a confirmation.

HISTORY
Conversations are kept on the phone across restarts and can be reopened from the home screen. History is sealed with a separate AES-256-GCM key that lives in the Android Keystore and never leaves the device, so a copy of the storage file is useless on another phone.

GOOD TO KNOW
* Both phones need the same version of the app.
* Bluetooth must be on and the phones within range.
* No internet, no accounts, no telemetry.
