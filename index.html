SecureBlue Chat

Encrypted Bluetooth chat. This page is an F-Droid repository.

  1. Open F-Droid and go to Settings → Repositories.
  2. Tap + and add https://sakurabun.github.io/securebluechat/repo.
  3. Refresh, search for SecureBlue Chat and install.

Source code: github.com/sakurabun/securebluechat
